= Getting involved and influencing CIM

There are a number of ways to get involved! 

* Join UCAIug @ [TBD]
* Join CIMug @ [TBD]

Once you've joined the appropriate user groups and task forces, you can easily:

* Access model & profile docs
* Submit real-world use cases 
* Participate in industry decision-making (emails, calls, meetings)
* Help shape next CIM editions


As a reminder, without incoming enthusiasts, experts, students, utilities, and others, the CIM standards development/evolution will slow. On the other hand, your presence will bring interest and improvements in topic areas that matter to you!
