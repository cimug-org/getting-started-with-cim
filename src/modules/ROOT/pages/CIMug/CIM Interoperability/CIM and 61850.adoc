= Where IEC 61850 fits

* Substation and device layer. IEC 61850 defines data models and services for protection, control, and automation at the substation and similar OT environments.
* Focused on IEDs, bays, and substation communication, with standardized logical nodes and fast event messaging (GOOSE, sampled values).
* Typically used by protection, SCADA, and substation engineering teams to ensure interoperable substation level behavior.
When utilities typically “need” IEC 61850
* Modernizing substation automation
* Multi vendor IED interoperability
* Fast, deterministic protection messaging
* Standardized substation data models
* Engineering/configuration tool unification
* Integrating legacy relays and new IEDs
* Transparent, future proof SCADA upgrades
* Harmonized OT semantics across sites
