= Welcome to UCAiug

The UCA International Users Group (UCAIug) is a non-profit corporation that provides a platform and a process for users and vendors of utility applications to contribute in the ongoing development of the CIM. The CIM is used as the basis for development of international standards for systems that need real-time data in the electric utility industry and other industries with similar requirements.

The UCAIug does not write international standards. Instead, it works closely with groups that do, such as the IEC Technical Committee 57, which focuses on how power systems manage and share information. The UCAIug provides tools to generate draft standards based on the CIM and a process for submitting draft standards into the IEC standards development process.
The UCAIug includes three main member groups:

* The CIM Users Group (xref:CIMug/CIMug.adoc[CIMug])
* The Open Field Message Bus (xref:OpenFMBug/OpenFMBug.adoc[OpenFMBug])
* The IEC61850 Users Group (xref:61850ug/61850ug.adoc[61850ug])

These groups are made up of utility companies and the vendors and consultants who supply them with applications, tools and services.
The goal of the UCAIug is to make sure different utility systems can work together, even if they are built by different companies. They support open standards that anyone can use. The UCAIug gives space for innovators to work together to:

* Support the development of open, public standards that meet the needs of the energy industry.
* Support testing programs to make sure different systems can work together in the field.
* Teach and promote the use of these standards so more people know about them.
* Support the use of these standards to build smarter grids worldwide.
